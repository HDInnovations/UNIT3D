# Vltava glossary

- **Work** — abstract cultural title independent of a concrete cut, translation, carrier, or encode. Examples: a film, series, album, game, or book.
- **Edition** — a distinct editorial or creative expression of a Work. Examples: theatrical cut, director's cut, restored edition, Czech dubbing release, fan edit, remaster, translation, or platform port.
- **Variant** — the technical representation of one Edition delivered by a torrent. Examples: UHD Blu-ray remux, WEB-DL, 1080p encode, FLAC rip, EPUB, or PC build.
- **Torrent** — the distribution artifact for one Variant, identified by its infohash and tracker state.
- **Source metadata** — bibliographic or catalogue information obtained from an external authority and confirmed by an uploader. It describes the Work or Edition, never silently a Variant.
- **Technical metadata** — facts measured from uploaded files or verified from release data. Examples: duration, video codec, resolution, HDR, audio tracks, bitrate, file size, source medium, language, subtitles, track count, checksum, and game build.
- **Community metadata** — Vltava-specific curatorial information. Examples: fan edit provenance, restoration notes, Czech localization quality, known defects, staff verification, and release notes.
